Portal Purpose & Overview

Attendance Management Portal

One central place to manage cross-project team staffing, mark daily shift attendance, and download executive reports.

Why We Built It
Engineers work across multiple client projects. Management previously had no single, reliable view of who is absent without info, who is on leave with task cover, who arrived late, and who is falling short of the 9-hour shift. Attendance was isolated in separate per-project spreadsheets with no history and no trends.
The portal provides one daily operational record for every team filled by the TL/PM, with automatic checks, an executive daily summary for management, and long-term trend analysis.
1. Central User Directory
Loads employees automatically from company Keycloak SSO. Search anyone easily and move engineers between projects in 1 click.
2. Daily Shifts & Leaves
Mark project attendance quickly, track exact check-in and check-out times, WFH, half-days, and pre-fill approved multi-day leaves automatically.
3. Reports, Trends & Excel Downloads
See daily summaries, review attendance trends (7 to 365 days or custom dates) with instant engineer search, and export formatted Excel sheets in 1 click.
Why We Built This

Common Problems in Team Attendance

Managing teams across multiple projects using spreadsheets creates confusion and wasted time.

1. No Proper Tracking of Check-In & Check-Out Times
Engineers check in and out at different times without accurate time records. Without timestamps, project leads cannot track punctuality, late arrivals, or total hours worked properly.
2. Scattered Spreadsheets & Hidden Bench Pool
Project leads kept isolated files. Management could not easily see available bench engineers for new project staffing.
3. Repeating the Same Leave Entry Every Day
When an engineer took an approved 5-day leave, the manager had to open the sheet and type "Leave" every single morning.
4. Missing Long-Term Attendance Trends
Management had no simple way to spot repeated late arrivals or compare team punctuality over months or weekdays.
5. Confusion Over Client Site vs. Head Office Duty
When engineers visited the Head Office for sprint reviews or IT setups, no clear note was recorded, creating confusion in timesheets.

Pre-Marking Approved Leaves in Advance

Enter pre-approved email leaves once; future dates are automatically marked while skipping weekends.

1. One-Time Setup
When leave is approved on email, the PM enters the date range (e.g. Oct 20 to Oct 24), leave type, and chooses a task cover person.
2. Skips Weekends Automatically
The system automatically skips Saturdays and Sundays. Only official working days are pre-marked as Leave (L).
3. Easy Early Return
If the engineer comes back to work early (e.g. Thursday), simply mark today as Present; remaining future leave days stay saved.

Location Transparency
Remarks on / & /summary

Client Site vs. Head Office Duty Remarks

Recording off-site work locations clearly so internal project timesheets remain 100% accurate.

The Real-World Situation
Engineers usually work at client sites. When visiting Head Office for sprint reviews, internal meetings, or IT hardware setup, they remain actively on duty for their project.
How Project Leads Mark It
1. Mark status as Present (P) on the daily grid.
2. Enter check-in and check-out times.
3. Type the simple reason in Remarks: "Visited Head Office for internal sprint review / IT hardware setup".
Where the Note Appears
• My Attendance (`/me`): Engineer can see their location note.
• Summary Dashboard (`/summary`): Visible in Present Members list.
• Daily Email Digest: Sent in executive daily summary email.
• Excel Downloads: Saved in the monthly log for timesheet records.

Security & Stack
Small Stack · Standard Security

Technology Stack & Security Architecture

Few moving parts, standard protocols, and enterprise-grade role-based access control.

Layer Technology & Implementation
Application Python 3.12, Flask 3.0, Gunicorn WSGI (--threads 8)
Pages & UI Server-rendered Jinja2 templates, inline dynamic SVG charts
Database SQLite 3 in container volume (WAL mode; PostgreSQL supported via DATABASE_URL)
Identity & SSO Keycloak, OpenID Connect Authorization Code Flow (Authlib)
Reports openpyxl for high-speed Excel export generation
Enterprise Security & Governance
• Zero Password Storage: Portal stores no credentials; authentication is 100% delegated to Keycloak.
• Single Sign-Out (SLO): Signing out in either app immediately terminates session across OpenProject & Keycloak via back-channel webhook.
• Dynamic 5-Minute Role Re-Check: Keycloak roles are refreshed every 5 minutes. A revoked role or disabled account loses access within 5 minutes.
• Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
  – Admin: Full control over all projects, settings, and bench pool.
  – TL / PM: Entry, leave modal, and summary for assigned projects only.
  – Engineer: Self-service personal attendance view (/me) only.
• CSRF & Session Security: Anti-CSRF token on every POST, HTTPS-only secure cookies.
• Configurable Settings: Shift start (default 09:30), 15m grace, 9h shift length, and weekly offs are settings, not hardcoded.
